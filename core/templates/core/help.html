Add transactions by typing or speaking

On the dashboard, write (or tap 🎤 and speak) in Bangla, English or Banglish: “আজ বাজারে ৫৫০ টাকা খরচ, tuition theke 6000 pelam”. AI splits it into transactions, picks categories and accounts, and shows them for you to review. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm & Save.

Debts

Say “Rahim ke 500 taka dhar dilam” and it goes to Debts, not to your expenses. Choose when to be reminded (on the day, or 1, 3 or 7 days before). Reminders appear under the 🔔 bell.

Accounts

You start with Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank and Card. Edit them, set the current balance, pick a bank or wallet logo, or add more on the Accounts page.

Ask HishabAI

Ask “how much did I spend on food last month?” or “bKash diye ki ki kinechi?”. You get the matching transactions with income, expense and balance. The numbers come straight from your records.

AI usage

Each person has a daily AI allowance (shown as “AI today: % used”). It resets on a rolling 24-hour basis. Manual entry, accounts, debts and reports never use it.
