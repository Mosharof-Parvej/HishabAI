What we store

  • Your email, name and a hashed password (or your Google sign-in email).
  • The transactions, accounts, debts, budgets and categories you enter.
  • If you subscribe: your name, email, phone number, payment details and the payment screenshot you upload. Only our staff can see these, and only to verify your payment.

AI features

When you use AI entry, Ask HishabAI or voice input, the text you typed, or the audio you recorded, is sent to our AI providers (Google Gemini and Groq) to be processed. For Ask AI, insights and budget tips we send summaries of your records without your name or email. Do not enter information you are not comfortable sending to these providers. Audio is not stored by HishabAI.

Your control

  • Export all transactions as CSV from Transactions or Settings.
  • Delete your account and all of its data from Settings at any time.

Cookies and visit counts

We use the cookies needed to keep you signed in and protect forms, plus one anonymous visitor cookie (hv, a random code) so we can count visitors and how long the app is used. It stores no IP address, no pages you opened, and nothing you typed or any amounts.

Not financial advice

HishabAI organises your own records. AI output can contain mistakes — check amounts before saving.
